## Repository-specific records

### D08 — FLORÉ: editorial structure, botanical warmth, painterly mass

**Use when:** When extending FLORÉ, its studio/template products, botanical archive, painting builder, typography, ornament library, or motion system.

[Recovered intent] FLORÉ combines orthographic structure, Memphis punctuation, illustrated botanical warmth, and impasto weight. The original handoff seeks a design-system showcase, template pack, and portfolio case. Retrieved reference-image text also names a Botanical Identity Archive, Painting Builder, Ornament Library, services, journal, case studies, and studio mode. Those labels restore a richer product horizon; they do not prove those screens are implemented.

[Design DNA] Preserve ivory/ink editorial contrast, blush, sage, lavender, ochre, deep green, large expressive serif hierarchy, spacious grids, asymmetrical painted panels, curated squiggles/dots/arches, and tactile surface work. Ornament follows hierarchy. Texture should read as authored paint/paper rather than generic screen noise.

[Observed drift] The README names botanical unfurl 60/15 and Memphis snap 250ms. Current src/data/motion.ts has gentle 120/18 and no named memphisSnap. The earlier research report identified the same discrepancy; this pass confirms that static mismatch. Original handoff fonts and later README font roles also differ, requiring a decision rather than a merged guess.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Keep orthography, decoration, illustration, paint, and interaction as separate controllable layers. Establish a component-state lab for Button, Card, PaintingPanel, MemphisDecor, Reveal, and composed sections. Calibrate named motion profiles side by side and update code/docs/Figma together. Choose real paint assets or CSS/SVG per fidelity, accessibility, editability, and cost.

[Reimagined horizon] A botanical editorial atelier: identities as specimens with context and lineage; a painting/ornament composer; reusable client templates; an inspectable studio process and bounded service offers.

[Carry-forward test] One identity specimen and one complete editorial page retain typographic order and botanical/painterly identity at mobile/desktop, keyboard, high contrast, reduced transparency, and reduced motion. The image reads in this pass yielded text/pointers; original pixels were not visually evaluated here.
